> ### Information of application.

AI assistance: ChatGPT 6.

Last update: 10/10/2026

**Idea**: Just like Calendar mix with alarm but can set a level of alarming, plans to has 4 levels: Chilling, Casual, Important and Emergency. Application volume and picture notification can be change to something funny like memes etc.

**Mode**:

- Chilling: Just a chilling sound play from application, low volume level.
- Casual: Like normal alarm, intermediate volume level.
- Important: Mode will build window frame with changable picture, intermediate volume level.
- Emergency: This gonna triggered you whether you doing anything which triggered frame which can't close until you input your password, high volume level.
